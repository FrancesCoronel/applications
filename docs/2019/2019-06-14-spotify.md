## What's your elevator pitch for your idea? (A short description of what it is)

Tech Queens is the first and only podcast that exclusively focuses on highlighting the stories and advice shared by women of color in tech.

## Please tell us more: description; structure; themes; types/examples of guests if applicable, audience, etc. If you have a pilot or existing episodes, please share, but totally fine if you don’t have anything beyond an idea.

Themes: Facing adversity, strategies used to overcome adversity, isolationism, resilience

Guests: Anyone who identifies as a woman of color working or studying in tech is welcome on the podcast. Interviewee Tracker: https://airtable.com/shr9yJfweA6scTesD

Audience: It caters to women of color working in tech or studying to be in the tech industry so they know they are not alone by being able to relate to the experiences of these successful women.

However, I also want men of color in tech and other allies to be able to listen to an episode and have a clear takeaway of "Oh, I have a better idea of what challenges women of color in tech face, why they are different from mine and how I can be supportive".

Existing Episodes: https://techqueenspod.com

Structure: The format for each 1-hour session is meant to be more serious and personal than most but ideally, we always end it a bit more light-heartedly.

## Why do you think the world needs to hear this podcast?

So equality wise - regardless of whatever statistics and numbers you look at, it’s very clear that the technology field is one of the leading growth industries in the US. The average salaries are over double the national average. And the perks offered to even an entry-level employee are vastly better when compared to almost every other industry.

Tech is also just very powerful - cloud computing, quantum computing, biotech, nanotech, big data, machine learning, drones - in the near future, all these have technologies will be embedded into our everyday lives and the creators of these technologies will be able to have a huge impact on those lives are managed.

Humans wield incredible power - the power to transform, to destroy and to create again. The question now is - do we want a small group of the population to be able to wield that power? I think not.
