## In a few sentences, tell us why this person is a Doer:

Frances is a Doer because there aren't enough stories out there highlighting leaders like Frances — someone with both an intersectional identity (a woman and also a person of color) and intersectional passions in both the technology and nonprofit space.

Techqueria is a 501c3 nonprofit that serves the largest community of Latinx in Tech and the work that Frances does as Techqueria's co-director is vital in making tech a more equitable place for people of all backgrounds by providing them opportunities to share resources and elevate each other. Techqueria has grown over 400% in weekly and daily active members over the past year to 1,000+ weekly active members and 600+ daily active members.

A lot of that success in engagement is owed to Frances's leadership in spearheading new technologies and ramping up new processes for them to scale as a nonprofit.

## Anything else we should know about them?

Frances is very vocal about her identity as a bad ass Latina in tech and is well known within the Latinx in Tech space through her contributions to Techqueria and for creating opportunities for others like her to make their stories more visible.

Frances is also the host of Tech Queens, the first podcast to focus exclusively on the stories and advice from women of color in tech and a huge proponent of open-source which is a field where very few women, let alone women of color, can be found thriving in and actively contributing to.
